What we do

Horse riding lessons for all ages, shapes, and sizes — from your first mount to graded showjumping and SAWMGA mounted games.

  • Learn to ride

    Private and group lessons on quiet school horses. Helmets and confidence-building for children, teens, and adults.

  • Showjumping & arena work

    Flatwork, poles, and courses that take you from basics to competition days at venues around Gauteng.

  • Mounted games & speed events

    Barrel turns, flags, and SAWMGA qualifiers for riders who love pace, precision, and teamwork with their horse.

  • Special moments

    Matric dances, portraits, and group rides — celebrate milestones with the horses at the centre.
