What we do

Horse riding for all ages and levels, hobby rider to competitive rider.

  • Learn to ride

    Lessons for all beginners, tiny tots to adults. Lessons from 2 years old.

  • Showjumping & arena work

    From the first pole to competition.

  • Western Mounted Games

    Ride for SANESA or SAWMGA

  • Competition

    Shows and teams through the year.

  • Outrides

    Come enjoy a 60-90 min ride out on the roads

  • Special moments

    Portraits for the days that matter.
